# Contoso Code of Conduct (LEG-POL-001)

Owner: Legal Department, Ethics and Compliance.

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from business partners worth up to 100 USD per person per year. Anything above 100 USD must be declined or registered in the Gifts Register and approved by Compliance. Gifts of cash or cash equivalents, such as gift cards, are never allowed. Government officials may not be offered any gifts.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest, such as a family member working for a supplier or a second job with a competitor, to your manager and Compliance within 30 days of it arising.

## Speaking up

Concerns about fraud, bribery, harassment or other misconduct can be reported to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics.contoso.com or by phone on 1-800-555-0199. Contoso does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Annual certification

All employees must complete Code of Conduct training and certify compliance every year by January 31.
