# Contoso Facilities and Workplace Safety Policy (FAC-POL-005)

Owner: Facilities Department.

## Office access

Offices are open from 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Employees must wear their badge visibly at all times. Out-of-hours access requires approval from Facilities at least 24 hours in advance. Lost badges must be reported to Reception the same day; replacement badges cost 15 USD after the first one.

## Desk booking

Desks are booked through the Contoso Spaces app up to 14 days in advance. Bookings not checked into by 10:00 are released automatically.

## Visitors

Visitors must be registered in Contoso Spaces before arrival, sign in at Reception and be escorted by their host at all times.

## Fire safety

When the fire alarm sounds, leave the building by the nearest exit, do not use the lifts, and go to the assembly point in the north car park. Fire drills are held twice a year. Each floor has trained fire wardens wearing yellow vests.

## Incidents and injuries

Report any workplace accident, injury or near miss to Facilities within 24 hours using the incident form on the intranet. First aid kits are located next to every kitchen.
