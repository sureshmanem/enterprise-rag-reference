# Contoso Learning and Development Policy (HR-POL-040)

Owner: HR Department, Learning and Development team.

## Training budget

Every employee has an individual training budget of 2,000 USD per calendar year for courses, conferences and certifications. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year. Requests are approved by the line manager in Workday Learning.

## Learning days

Employees may take 5 paid learning days per year to attend training. Learning days are separate from annual leave.

## Tuition reimbursement

Contoso reimburses up to 10,000 USD per year in tuition for degree programmes related to the employee's role. Employees who leave Contoso within 12 months of a reimbursement must repay 100% of it; between 12 and 24 months, 50% must be repaid.

## Mandatory training

Information security awareness training must be completed within 30 days of joining and every year after that. Managers must complete the Leading at Contoso programme within 6 months of becoming a people manager.
