# Contoso Travel and Expenses Policy (FIN-POL-007)

## Meals

The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is 75 USD. For international travel the daily meal allowance is 100 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Hotels

Hotel bookings must be made through the Concur travel portal. The nightly hotel cap is 250 USD in major cities and 175 USD elsewhere.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in Concur within 30 days of the trip with itemised receipts for every expense above 25 USD. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.
