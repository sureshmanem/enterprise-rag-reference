# Contoso Procurement Policy (FIN-POL-012)

Owner: Finance Department, Procurement team.

## Purchase orders

A purchase order (PO) must be raised in Coupa before any goods or services are ordered. Invoices without a valid PO number are returned to the supplier. Personal credit cards must not be used for purchases from suppliers.

## Approval thresholds

Purchases up to 5,000 USD are approved by the budget owner. Purchases between 5,000 USD and 50,000 USD also need approval from the department head. Purchases above 50,000 USD also need approval from the CFO.

## Competitive quotes

At least three written quotes are required for any purchase above 10,000 USD. A single-source purchase above 10,000 USD requires a written justification approved by Procurement.

## New suppliers

New suppliers are onboarded through Coupa and must pass a sanctions screening and a security assessment before the first PO is issued. Supplier onboarding usually takes 10 working days.

## Payment terms

Contoso's standard payment terms are 45 days from the date a correct invoice is received.
