# Contoso Leave Policy (HR-POL-014)

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees receive annual leave pro-rated to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to request leave

Submit leave requests in the Workday portal at least 2 weeks in advance for annual leave. Your line manager approves or declines the request within 3 working days.
