# Contoso IT Acceptable Use Policy (IT-POL-003)

Owner: IT Department. Applies to all employees, contractors and interns using Contoso devices, networks or accounts.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across the last 10 passwords. Passwords are changed only when a compromise is suspected; there is no scheduled expiry. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, Workday and Concur. Never share a password or approve an MFA prompt you did not start.

## Software

Only software from the Contoso Company Portal may be installed on company laptops. Requests for other software are raised as an IT service desk ticket and reviewed by the security team within 5 working days. Browser extensions that read page content require security approval.

## Data storage

Company data must be stored in OneDrive or SharePoint, never on USB drives or personal cloud accounts. USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops. Personal use of company devices is allowed if it is occasional and does not conflict with this policy.

## Monitoring

Contoso monitors company networks and devices for security purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT service desk within 24 hours by calling extension 4357 (HELP). IT will remotely wipe the device.
