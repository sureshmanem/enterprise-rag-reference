# Contoso Data Privacy and Classification Policy (LEG-POL-002)

Owner: Legal Department, Privacy Office.

## Data classification

All company information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer personal data and employee HR records are classified as Restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit and may only be shared with named individuals.

## Personal data

Contoso processes personal data in line with GDPR and applicable local law. Collect only the personal data needed for a stated purpose. Customer personal data is retained for 6 years after the end of the customer relationship and then deleted.

## Data subject requests

Requests from individuals to access, correct or delete their data must be forwarded to privacy@contoso.com immediately. The Privacy Office responds within 30 days.

## Data breaches

Report any suspected data breach to the Privacy Office within 24 hours of discovery. The Privacy Office decides whether to notify the regulator, which must happen within 72 hours of Contoso becoming aware of a reportable breach.

## Sharing with vendors

Personal data may only be shared with vendors who have signed a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) reviewed by Legal.
