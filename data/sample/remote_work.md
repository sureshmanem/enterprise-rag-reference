# Contoso Remote Work Guidelines (HR-POL-021)

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.

## Equipment

Contoso provides a laptop, a monitor and a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## Security

Always connect through the corporate VPN when working remotely. Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 24 hours.
