# Contoso Employee Benefits Overview (HR-POL-030)

Owner: HR Department, Total Rewards team. Applies to full-time employees in the United States.

## Health insurance

Contoso pays 90% of medical insurance premiums for employees and 75% for dependants. Dental and vision coverage are fully paid by Contoso. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

## Retirement

Contoso matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 5% of base salary. Company matching contributions vest immediately.

## Wellness

Each employee receives a wellness allowance of 600 USD per year for gym memberships, fitness classes or mental health apps. Claims are submitted in Workday and paid with the next payroll.

## Employee assistance programme

The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free, confidential counselling of up to 6 sessions per issue per year for employees and their household members.

## Enrolment

New hires must enrol in benefits within 30 days of their start date. Changes outside the annual open enrolment period in November are only allowed after a qualifying life event.
